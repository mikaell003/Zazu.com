About Metadata

Metadata is hidden information stored in files that can reveal personal details like:

  • Photos: Location, camera model, date taken
  • Documents: Author name, edit history
  • PDFs: Creation software, modification dates
  • Audio: Album info, recording device

This tool helps protect your privacy by stripping this hidden data from your files.
