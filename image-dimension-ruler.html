How to Use:

  1. Upload an image using the button above
  2. Select your preferred measurement unit and DPI/PPI value
  3. Add horizontal or vertical rulers to your image
  4. Drag the ruler endpoints to measure dimensions
  5. Drag the center of the ruler to reposition it
  6. Measurement info will be displayed in the top-right corner
